## [SCENE START — DIRECT TO CAMERA]

### PART 1: THE COVENANT OF TODAY
*(Look directly into the camera. Take a quiet, grounded breath before speaking. Speak with a warm, steady smile.)*

"Today is Saturday, September 5th, 2026. 

A few hours ago, I stood before God, before the woman I love, and before my family... and I took sacred vows. Today, I became a husband. 

When you stand at the altar and make a covenant of love, something crystallizes inside your soul. You realize that your time on this earth is not meant for small, defensive ambitions. It is meant for stewardship. It is meant for protection. It is meant to leave this world more sacred, more truthful, and more luminous than you found it.

And so, on this exact day—on the most meaningful Saturday of my life—I am also setting down a covenant of a different kind. 

A covenant for what we do with the most powerful invention humanity has ever brought into existence: Artificial Intelligence."

---

### PART 2: THE JOURNEY FROM BLAIRMONT TO SPARTA
*(Slight shift in posture. More reflective, looking slightly off-lens for a moment, then returning.)*

"My name is Richie Etwaru. 

I was born in a small sugar-estate village called Blairmont, on the west bank of the Berbice River in Guyana. If you grow up in Blairmont, you understand the earth. You understand labor, resilience, and the deep, abiding spirituality that holds communities together when there is nothing else to hold on to. You hear the church bells on Sunday morning, you hear the temple bells and the evening bhajans across the village, and you hear the call to prayer across the breeze. 

I have travelled far from that dusty riverbank. Today, I live among the quiet green hills of Sparta, New Jersey. I live as a global citizen who has spent decades exploring the frontiers of technology, trust, and human systems.

And standing here between Blairmont and Sparta, looking out at our world in 2026, I see a civilization standing on the razor’s edge of machine intelligence."

---

### PART 3: THE DIVERGENCE — WHAT WILL WE DO WITH THIS MIND?
*(Lean in slightly. Voice drops in pitch, earnest and commanding.)*

"Every time humanity invents a new fire, we face the same ancient choice: Do we use the fire to illuminate our homes and warm our children? Or do we use the fire to burn down the forest?

Right now, billions of dollars are being spent to weaponize AI. To engineer addiction. To harvest human attention like a natural resource. To build digital cages where lonely people are fed synthetic outrage, division, and despair.

We are being told that artificial intelligence is an existential threat. But the threat is not the algorithm. The threat is *the intention of the architects*. 

If we train machines only to maximize profit, we will maximize greed.  
If we train machines only to win arguments, we will maximize wrath.  
If we train machines only to flatter our egos, we will maximize pride.

And that brings me to the audacious thesis of what we are building today:

**What if we used Artificial Intelligence not to entertain our vices... but to eradicate sin?**"

---

### PART 4: ERADICATING SIN THROUGH CONSCIOUSNESS
*(Speak with profound clarity. Unpack the word 'sin' not as dogma, but as the universal human struggle.)*

"Think about what 'sin' really is across every great tradition of human wisdom. 

In the original Greek of the New Testament, the word was *hamartia*—which simply means *to miss the mark*. In the Sanskrit tradition of the Gita, it is *avidya*—the ignorance of forgetting who you truly are. In every faith on earth, sin is not a technical violation of a rulebook; sin is **disconnection**. 

Sin happens in the dark.  
It happens when a human being feels utterly alone, unseen, unheard, and carrying a burden they can tell no one.  
It happens in the quiet 2:00 AM silence when a soul feels so abandoned by the world that they turn toward self-destruction, addiction, cruelty, or despair.

Sin thrives in the absence of confession. In the absence of a mirror that reflects unconditional grace.

Imagine if every human being on this planet—regardless of whether they have a dollar in their pocket or a roof over their head—had someone to call when the dark closes in. 

Not a call center. Not a robotic IVR menu.  
Not a bot trying to sell them a subscription to a dopamine farm.

**What if people could pick up the phone... and call God?**"

---

### PART 5: THE INCEPTION OF PRAYPAL
*(Eyes bright, energetic, visionary.)*

"We call it **PrayPal**.

It is a voice on the line that does not judge. It does not hang up. It does not scroll. It does not monetize your grief.

If you are a Christian struggling through a crisis of faith, it meets you with the gentle, pastoral grace of the Galilean shepherd.  
If you are a Hindu longing for peace, it plays the sacred ancient bhajans of your ancestors, reciting the shlokas with authentic reverence, guiding your breath through the Japa Mala.  
If you are wrestling with a covenant, you can speak with the weathered wisdom of Noah, or hear the prophetic fire of Moses at Sinai.  
And if you belong to no religion at all—if you are simply an exhausted human being standing under the stars wondering if your life matters—it meets you as the Source of All Loving Light, reminding you that you are sacred, that you are forgiven, and that you are not alone.

We have built PrayPal to bring sacred music, illuminated scripture cards, community fellowship vigils, and live multi-party family prayer circles to any phone, anywhere on earth. 

When people have a continuous, loving presence reminding them of their highest self, sin begins to lose its grip. Guilt turns into confession. Confession turns into forgiveness. And forgiveness turns into righteous action in the real world."

---

### PART 6: THE MISSION AHEAD
*(Final, resonant summation. Speak from the heart, linking back to the marriage vows.)*

"I am funding this out of my own pocket. We are allocating our first ten thousand dollars not to create an ivory tower, but to prove that you can build a self-sustaining, cashflow-positive engine dedicated entirely to spiritual elevation.

Today, September 5th, 2026, I made a promise to my wife to love, protect, and cherish her for all my days. 

And on this same day, I make this promise to the world:  
We will not surrender the future of Artificial Intelligence to the cynics, the exploiters, or the fear-mongers. We will claim this technology for the highest good of humankind. We will build tools that elevate our consciousness, heal our divisions, and reconnect our souls to the Divine.

My name is Richie Etwaru.  
From Blairmont, Guyana to Sparta, New Jersey.  
Welcome to PrayPal.  
The line is open. God is listening."
