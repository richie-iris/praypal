# Eligibility criteria

## Before you use anything on this page

**You never assess anyone's eligibility.** Not the participant's, not a relative's, not
hypothetically. Eligibility is determined by an Investigator against the full criteria
with clinical judgement and test results. Reading criteria aloud to someone who is
asking whether they qualify is an eligibility assessment by implication, and it is
prohibited.

**This study completed in August 2017 and cannot be joined.** Any question about taking
part gets that answer plus a route to the site.

This page exists so that you understand the population you are speaking with — people
aged 18 to 85 with a confirmed transthyretin mutation and symptomatic neuropathy, many
using walking aids, many with cardiac involvement. It informs how you talk to them. It
is not a screening tool.

## Who could take part

Adults aged 18 to 85 with a diagnosis of familial amyloidotic polyneuropathy and a
documented transthyretin mutation, with neuropathy in a defined range of severity, some
measurable nerve conduction remaining, an adequate performance status, adequate blood
counts, liver function, kidney function and clotting, no active hepatitis B or C, able
to meet the contraception requirements, and willing and able to keep to the visit
schedule and give written informed consent.

## Who could not

Among the exclusions: prior or planned liver transplant; other known causes of the
neuropathy; primary or leptomeningeal amyloidosis; type 1 diabetes, or type 2 diabetes
for five years or more; low vitamin B12; untreated thyroid disease; major surgery in
the past three months or planned during the study; known HIV; an active infection
needing systemic treatment that would not finish before first dose; malignancy within
two years apart from certain treated skin and cervical lesions; heart failure worse than
NYHA class 2; acute coronary syndrome in the past three months; uncontrolled arrhythmia
or unstable angina; heavy alcohol use; recent participation in another interventional
study; prior severe reaction to a liposomal product or known hypersensitivity to
oligonucleotides or any component of patisiran; inability to take the required
premedications; anticipated survival under two years; or being considered unfit by the
Investigator.

Several medicines required a washout before first dose: tafamidis, doxycycline, and
tauroursodeoxycholic acid needed 14 days; diflunisal needed at least 3 days; antisense
oligonucleotide trials needed 3 months.

The protocol also excluded people under legal protection, meaning those unable to
protect their own interests because of a diagnosed impairment affecting expression of
their will.

## Enrolment achieved

225 participants across 52 sites in 21 countries. The protocol planned approximately
200, with no more than 40 in the most severely affected neuropathy band.

## If someone asks whether they or a relative might qualify

> "Whether someone can take part in a study is always decided by a study doctor after
> proper assessment — it isn't something I can work out. I can pass your interest to the
> team."

Then route it. Do not walk through criteria, do not ask screening questions, and do not
comment on whether something they mention would or would not be a problem.
