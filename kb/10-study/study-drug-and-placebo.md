# The study drug and the placebo

## Patisiran

Patisiran (development code ALN-TTR02) is an RNA interference therapeutic. It contains
a small interfering RNA directed at transthyretin messenger RNA, both the mutant and
the normal form, packaged in a lipid nanoparticle so that it reaches the liver.

The intent is to reduce how much transthyretin protein the liver produces. Explaining
that is fine. Asserting that it will help this participant is not — that is the question
the study is testing.

It is supplied as a sterile solution containing 2 mg/mL of the siRNA, in 10 mL glass
vials, and is diluted into a bag of sterile normal saline before use.

## Dose

0.3 mg/kg, given intravenously once every 21 days, with a window of ±3 days.

The dose is calculated from the participant's body weight recorded at the **previous**
visit. If that weight is unavailable, the weight taken before dosing on the day is used
instead. Participants weighing 105 kg or more are dosed on an assumed body weight of
104 kg — that is a protocol-defined cap, not an error, and it is the pharmacy's
calculation to make, never yours.

## Placebo

Sterile normal saline, 0.9% sodium chloride, given intravenously on the same schedule,
at the same rate, in the same total volume, with the same premedication.

If a participant asks what a placebo is: it is a preparation with no active medicine in
it, used so the study can tell what is caused by the medicine and what would have
happened anyway. That is a factual answer and you may give it. What you must never do
is discuss which they might be receiving.

## Total volume and appearance

Every infusion, in either arm, is 200 mL total. Bags and lines are covered with amber
sleeves before leaving the pharmacy. Infusion equipment must be free of PVC and DEHP,
and an in-line 1.2 micron filter is used.

## Handling and storage

Study drug is stored refrigerated at approximately 5 ± 3 °C, upright, in a secure
temperature-controlled location, and is dispensed only by authorised staff. It is never
given to anyone not enrolled, never used for any other purpose, and never redispensed
once returned.

Participants do not handle, store, or transport study drug. It is prepared and given
entirely at the site. If a participant asks about taking it home, the answer is that it
is given only at the study site by the study team.
